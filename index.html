What Are Score Functions? (click to expand)

Score functions are the mathematical transformations that convert raw data into a form used by nonparametric tests. They are the backbone of nonparametric inference.

Ranks

Replace values with their position in sorted order

a(i) = i
Signs

Indicate direction of difference (+ or −)

sign(dᵢ) = ±1
Pairwise

Compare all pairs of observations

sign(Xⱼ−Xᵢ)
Survival

Event probabilities over time

Ŝ(t) = Π(1−dᵢ/nᵢ)
Resampling

Repeated sampling with replacement

B = 1000 iterations
Smoothing

Local weighted averages

K((x−xᵢ)/h)

Why Do Score Functions Matter?

  • ✅ Distribution-free: Scores don't depend on the underlying distribution
  • ✅ Robust: Outliers have limited influence (bounded by rank)
  • ✅ Interpretable: Scores reflect relative ordering, not absolute values
  • ✅ Powerful: Often maintain high efficiency vs parametric counterparts
